The brief

Homesearch Properties wanted to scale its managed portfolio by buying established lettings and property management businesses, rather than relying on organic growth alone.

Businesses of the right size rarely come to market openly, and the ones that do attract competing buyers.

02

What we did

  • Mapped lettings and property management businesses that matched the target size.
  • Approached owners directly across LinkedIn, email and direct mail.
  • Qualified each conversation on portfolio size and the owner's appetite to sell.
  • Passed on only the opportunities that fitted the mandate.
03

The result

Twenty off-market opportunities in six months, each a business managing between 50 and 150 properties.

None were listed with a broker, giving Homesearch Properties first conversations with owners before any competing buyer.
