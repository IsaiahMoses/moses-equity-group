Privacy notice.

How Moses Equity Group collects, uses and protects personal data. Last updated October 2026.

Who we are

Moses Equity Group LTD ("we", "us") is a UK company that sources off-market acquisition opportunities in the property management and lettings sector and introduces business owners to potential acquirers. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this notice. You can contact us at isaiah@mosesequitygroup.co.uk.

What data we collect

  • Business contact details: name, job title, company, business email address, business phone number and LinkedIn profile.
  • Information about your business, such as its size, location and publicly filed company information.
  • Records of our communications with you, including emails and messages.

Where we get it from

Directly from you, and from publicly available sources such as Companies House, company websites, business directories and LinkedIn.

How we use it and our lawful basis

We use this information to contact business owners and acquirers about potential sale or acquisition opportunities, to qualify those opportunities, to make introductions and to manage our relationship with you. We rely on our legitimate interests in identifying and facilitating business sales and acquisitions. Where we need your consent, for example for certain electronic marketing, we will ask for it.

Who we share it with

We only share information about you or your business with potential buyers or sellers with your agreement, and usually only after they have signed a confidentiality agreement. We also use trusted service providers, such as email, CRM and outreach software, who process data on our behalf. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. If you ask us not to contact you, we keep a minimal record so that we do not contact you again.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to object to direct marketing at any time. To exercise any of these rights, or to stop hearing from us, email isaiah@mosesequitygroup.co.uk.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
